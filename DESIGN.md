## Overview

ROAD TO 43 presents the 43rd anniversary celebration of Remas Al Munawwar as a festival landing page paired with a short interactive marble adventure. Its visual direction combines editorial event typography and bright, playful festival graphics.

## Typography

Use DM Sans for general reading and primary headings, Space Grotesk for identity and compact display labels, and italic Lora as a restrained editorial accent.

## Layout

Keep event information available independently of the WebGL experience. The site adapts its layout and touch controls for mobile, and respects reduced-motion preferences by disabling ambient page animation.
